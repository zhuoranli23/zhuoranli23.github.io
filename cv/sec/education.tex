\section{Research Interests}
I am broadly interested in \textbf{robot learning}, \textbf{planning}, and \textbf{agentic robotics}. My goal is to build general-purpose robots that can carry out complex, long-horizon tasks and adapt their plans when conditions change. I'd like these robots to take on work ranging from routine household chores to hazardous jobs on the factory floor. I favor simple methods that scale with data and compute.
